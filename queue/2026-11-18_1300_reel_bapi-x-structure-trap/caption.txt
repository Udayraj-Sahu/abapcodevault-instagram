Your BAPI said success, but nothing changed? Check the X structure.

1. Change BAPIs (e.g. BAPI_MATERIAL_SAVEDATA) pass data in pairs: CLIENTDATA + CLIENTDATAX
2. The X structure has one flag per field; 'X' means: change this field
3. Value without its X flag = the field is ignored
4. After the BAPI, call BAPI_TRANSACTION_COMMIT

Comment XFLAG if this ever cost you an hour of debugging.
