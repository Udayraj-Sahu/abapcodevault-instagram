Working in SD? These 10 tables come up in almost every report and enhancement.

Order: VBAK, VBAP, VBEP, VBKD, VBPA
Flow between documents: VBFA
Delivery: LIKP, LIPS
Billing: VBRK, VBRP

The key that ties them together is the document number plus item number, and VBFA tells you which delivery and invoice belong to which order.
