"What is the difference between a domain and a data element?"

A simple way to answer:
- Domain: the technical side. Data type, length, decimals, fixed values, conversion routine.
- Data element: the meaning. Field labels, F1 documentation, search help. It refers to a domain.

Then give a real example, such as KUNNR with its ALPHA conversion. That one detail shows you have actually worked in SE11.
