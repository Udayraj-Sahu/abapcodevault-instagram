Still building a field catalog for every simple report?

CL_SALV_TABLE gives you a full ALV in a few lines:
1. factory( ) with your internal table
2. get_functions( )->set_all( ) for sort, filter and Excel export
3. display( )

Columns and headings come from the table type. Wrap the call in TRY...CATCH cx_salv_msg.

Which ALV do you use most: SALV or REUSE_ALV_GRID_DISPLAY?
