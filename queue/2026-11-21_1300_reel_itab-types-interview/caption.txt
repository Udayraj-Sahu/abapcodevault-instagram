"STANDARD, SORTED or HASHED?" comes up in almost every ABAP interview.

1. STANDARD: index access, linear search by key. Fine for small tables and ALV output.
2. SORTED: kept sorted by its key, binary search, supports partial keys.
3. HASHED: full unique key only, constant read time at any size.

Which one do you use inside loops?
