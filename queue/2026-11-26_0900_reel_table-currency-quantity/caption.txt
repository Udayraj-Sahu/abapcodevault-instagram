SAP ABAP tables, Part 9 of 18: currency and quantity fields in SE11.

1. A CURR field needs a reference field of type CUKY (the currency)
2. A QUAN field needs a reference field of type UNIT (the unit)
3. Set both on the Currency/Quantity Fields tab: reference table + reference field
4. Example: VBAK-NETWR points to WAERK, VBAP-KWMENG to VRKME
5. Common mistake: a plain CHAR field as the partner
