Where should a fresher start? Not with RAP.

How to learn SAP ABAP in the right order, as an ABAP fresher:

1. Basics: SAP GUI, T-codes, a first report in SE38
2. Language: variables, data types, loops
3. Data Dictionary: domains and tables in SE11
4. Reports: ABAP SQL, internal tables, ALV
5. Modern: classes, CDS views, then RAP
