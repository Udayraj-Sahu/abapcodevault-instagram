Is your report taking 20 minutes instead of 2?

In most cases the cause is the same: a SELECT inside a LOOP. Every iteration is a separate trip to the database.

The fix: read the data once before the loop (JOIN or FOR ALL ENTRIES), then look it up in an internal table inside the loop. For large volumes, use a SORTED or HASHED table.
