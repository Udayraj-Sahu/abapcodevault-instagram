A classic SAP ABAP BAPI bug: no BAPI_TRANSACTION_COMMIT.

1. Most BAPIs only queue the change, they don't commit
2. Check RETURN for type E messages
3. No errors: BAPI_TRANSACTION_COMMIT with WAIT = abap_true
4. Errors: BAPI_TRANSACTION_ROLLBACK
5. Without WAIT, the next read may not see the document yet
