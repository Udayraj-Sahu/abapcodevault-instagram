Stop guessing what 1:CN means.

SAP ABAP tables, Part 14 of 18: foreign key cardinality in SE11.

1. Cardinality = how many rows on each side of a foreign key belong together
2. Left side: 1 (always a match) or C (the field may be empty)
3. Right side: 1, C (zero or one), N (one or more), CN (any number)
4. Example: ZTAIRLINE to T005 is 1:CN
5. It documents the link, it doesn't block data
