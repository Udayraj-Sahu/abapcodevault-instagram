A user reports an error, and you have no idea which line of code raised it.

The fastest way to find out:
1. Type /h in the command field and press Enter
2. Repeat the action to open the debugger
3. Breakpoints > Breakpoint at Statement > MESSAGE
4. Press F8. The debugger stops at the exact line.

Look at the condition above that line. That is usually your root cause.

Save this for your next production issue.
