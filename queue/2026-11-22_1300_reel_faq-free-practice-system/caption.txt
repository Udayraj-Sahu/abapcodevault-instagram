Stop paying for SAP: practise for free in 4 steps.

How to get a free SAP system to practise ABAP, the official way:

1. Sign up for the free SAP BTP trial
2. In the trial cockpit, run the booster "Prepare an Account for ABAP Trial"
3. Install Eclipse with ABAP Development Tools (ADT)
4. Connect to the trial system and write your first class

Good to know: it is ABAP Cloud, with no SAP GUI. SAP has announced the end of the Docker developer trial. The BTP trial is time-limited: log in regularly.
