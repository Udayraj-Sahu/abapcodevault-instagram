Your READ TABLE can't find customer 1000? It's right there in the table.

Myth or fact: BINARY SEARCH always makes READ TABLE faster on an internal table.

1. Binary search checks the middle row and throws half the table away
2. On an unsorted table it can throw away the half with your row: sy-subrc 4
3. Fix: SORT the table BY the same field you search with
4. Or use a SORTED table, it searches by its key for you
